Show Game Instructions

How to Play:
- Guess a number between 1 and 500.
- You have 10 tries to guess the correct number.
- After each guess, you'll be told if your guess is too high or too low.
- If you guess correctly, you win!
- If you use all 10 tries, you lose and the correct number is revealed.
- Click "Restart" to play again.
